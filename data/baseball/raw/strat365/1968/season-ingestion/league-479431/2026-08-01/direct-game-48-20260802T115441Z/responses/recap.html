Tony Perez had 4 hits at Shibe Park 1968 where the Ohio Players beat the Aquarium Drinkers 4 to 2.

Ohio started with a bang, jumping out to an early lead in their half of the first scoring 2 runs using 2 hits. After two were out Frank Robinson keyed the rally when he reached on an error. Roberto Clemente was next to the plate and he smashed a two-run homer (his 3rd of the season). Aquarium had a chance to come back in the 9th but they came up short.

Larry Dierker(2-0) went 8 innings allowing 1 run for the victory. Dierker got help from Hoyt Wilhelm who earned his 4th save. Jim Nash(1-1) suffered the loss. He pitched 7 innings giving up 10 hits and 1 walk.

Joe Ginto was in a contemplative mood after the tough loss, 'It is really hard to lose this kind of a game. It may seem weird to some, but it is easier to take the occasional blow-out loss. It is the ones that just barely get away that hurt so much - like this one!'
