In a dramatic ending at Fulton Stadium 1968 the Iowa bombers won in their last at-bat beating the Aquarium Drinkers by the count of 3 to 2.

The game was decided in the bottom of the 9th inning. Ron Santo slapped a base-hit. After an out was recorded, Ted Uhlaender stepped in and he drew a walk. Tom Haller got his turn next, but he was a strikeout victim. Jim Lefebvre followed and he laced a one-base hit resulting in an exciting win for Iowa and a wild post-game celebration by the home town fans. Iowa banged out 7 hits on the afternoon.

The victory went to Dick Selma(2-5) who went 1 inning, allowing no runs. The loss was charged to Darold Knowles(0-3) in relief. He gave up 2 hits and 1 walk in 2/3 of an inning.
