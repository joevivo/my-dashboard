Roberto Clemente blasted a homerun at Comiskey Park 1968 as the Ohio Players beat the Aquarium Drinkers 5 to 3.

Don Drysdale(1-3) earned his pay with a solid performance for Ohio. He surrendered 8 hits and 3 walks in 7 innings improving his ERA from 4.98 to 4.40. Ohio out-hit Aquarium for the game, 12 hits to 9.

Hoyt Wilhelm recorded the save, his 6th. Jim Nash(1-2) was tagged with the loss. He allowed a few too many baserunners giving up 9 hits and 3 walks in 6 and 2/3 innings.

'I was a little good, a little bad and a little ugly.' said Drysdale, 'I was just giving us a chance to win.'
